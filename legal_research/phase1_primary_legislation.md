## SECTION 11: SUMMARY OF GAPS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

### Gaps Identified

1. **Civil Procedure Code (Arabic/French full text):** No freely available consolidated full-text online; Scribd has an Arabic upload but provenance is unclear; full text requires subscription (Lexis Middle East) or library access (Al-Mustashar).

2. **Military Justice Code (full text):** Only a metadata/summary reference found (ICRC); no freely available full text located online. May be available via Lebanese University Legiliban or official military tribunal sources.

3. **Personal Status Laws:** Lebanon operates 15+ separate sectarian personal status systems (Sunni, Shia, Druze, Maronite, Greek Orthodox, etc.). There is no centralized online database of all personal status laws. Individual religious community codes would require searching each denomination's courts and institutions separately.

4. **Municipal Bylaws:** No centralized online database; must be sought from individual municipalities or via Official Gazette subscription.

5. **Decree-Laws 1943–1990 (comprehensive):** Individually scattered; no single free online index; Legiliban and Al-Mustashar hold the most complete collections but require institutional/subscription access.

6. **Data Protection Legislation (standalone):** Lebanon's data protection provisions are embedded within Law No. 81/2018 (E-Transactions); no standalone comprehensive data protection law had been enacted as of the research date. Monitor the Official Gazette for new data protection legislation.

7. **Post-2020 Constitutional Amendments:** No formal constitutional amendment has been enacted post-2020 (crisis period); Constitute Project shows 2004 as last formal revision; monitor lp.gov.lb for any new constitutional laws.

8. **Capital Controls Legislation:** Lebanon has operated under de facto informal capital controls since 2019; formal legislation remains contested. Monitor Official Gazette and BDL circulars closely.

### Priority Recommendations for AI Database

1. **Start with Official Gazette subscription** (jo.pcm.gov.lb) — the foundational authentic source
2. **Mirror BDL Laws database** (bdl.gov.lb/laws.php) — best structured official source for banking/financial legislation
3. **Negotiate institutional access** to Al-Mustashar (المستشار في القانون اللبناني) — the most comprehensive searchable database
4. **Archive Infopro PDFs immediately** — direct links (data.infopro.com.lb) may be unstable; wget/mirror recommended
5. **Index CYRILLA collection** for digital rights / human rights laws
6. **Index Refworld** for nationality and foreigners' laws
7. **Map AML/banking law evolution** using ABL amendment tracker as the spine
