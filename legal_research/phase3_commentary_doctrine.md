## RESEARCH GAPS AND LIMITATIONS

1. **Mجلة القضائية (SADER)** full online archive requires subscription to Saderlex — no confirmed free digital access to individual issues.

2. **Revue Juridique Libanaise** — Limited online presence; primarily circulated in print; only sample issues found online (LAGBD).

3. **USEK Revue Juridique** — No confirmed open-access digital portal identified; articles accessible only through institutional subscription.

4. **USJ Annales de la Faculté de Droit** — Pre-1970 issues digitized by Google Books/University of Michigan; later issues institutional only.

5. **Lebanese University Journal** — Online portal (droit.ul.edu.lb/journal) returned a 404 error during research; accessible via dspa.ul.edu.lb.

6. **Religious court publications** — Islamic personal status court decisions and Maronite ecclesiastical court rulings are generally not published online; Maronite canonical law is explicitly noted as "non-published and secretly held" in historical scholarship.

7. **Tripoli Bar Association journal** — Limited digital presence identified.

8. **Conference proceedings** — Most Lebanese legal conference proceedings are not systematically digitized or archived online.

9. **NDU dedicated law review** — No dedicated law journal identified at Notre Dame University – Louaize; faculty publish in other Lebanese and international journals.

10. **LAU law review** — No dedicated law review journal identified at LAU; research output is scattered across individual scholars' profiles.

## KEY LEBANESE LEGAL SCHOLARS REFERENCED

The following scholars are frequently cited in Lebanese legal doctrine and commentaries:

- **Judge Dr. Mustafa El-Aougi (1929-2012)** — Standard authority on Lebanese civil and penal law; 15+ treatises; official archive at mustafa-aougi.org
- **Judge Atef Al-Naqib (عاطف النقيب)** — Leading author on Lebanese civil procedure and civil law; published by SADER
- **Judge Samir Alia** — Leading author on Lebanese penal law
- **Prof. Pierre Gannagé** — USJ (cited in USEK Revue Juridique; authority on Lebanese private international law and personal status)
- **Ibrahim Najjar** — Editor of Lebanese Review of Arab and International Arbitration; former Lebanese Minister of Justice
- **Nizar Saghieh** — Executive Director, Legal Agenda; Yale-trained; leading Lebanese constitutional law voice
- **Lara Eid Jreissati** — Author of GlobaLex Lebanese Legal System guide (2025)
