## PART VIII: KEY GAPS AND RESEARCH NOTES

**Gaps Identified:**
1. **No comprehensive Lebanese NGO database** of court decisions exists; Legal Agenda is the closest to this function but remains primarily news/analysis
2. **ALDIC's electoral law** publications are primarily anti-corruption focused; dedicated electoral law analysis is less developed
3. **MARCH Lebanon** produces minimal legal publications; primarily programmatic
4. **Brookings Institution** lacks a dedicated Lebanon legal program; analysis is episodic
5. **Carnegie MEC Diwan** is primarily short-form analysis; deeper legal research requires using Carnegie's parent database

**Cross-referenced valuable resources discovered during research:**
- **The Policy Initiative** (https://www.thepolicyinitiative.org): emerging governance/legal analysis organization closely connected to Legal Agenda
- **Gherbal Initiative**: ATI law monitoring (unique empirical data)
- **CLDH (Lebanese Center for Human Rights)**: significant publications on torture, detention, legal aid
- **Arab Reform Initiative**: https://www.arab-reform.net — migrant domestic workers analysis, regional reform
- **TIMEP (Tahrir Institute for Middle East Policy)**: Lebanon access to information, UPR joint submissions
- **Frontiers Rights**: mentioned in civil society coalitions; human rights legal support
